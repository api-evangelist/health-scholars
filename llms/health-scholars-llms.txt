# Health Scholars (UpSkillAI)

> Health Scholars rebranded to UpSkillAI in 2025 and now trades at upskillai.com. It builds
> AI-powered extended reality (XR/VR) simulation training for mission-critical, hands-on work in
> healthcare, emergency medical services, stroke assessment and defense. Voice-driven VR
> simulations are paired with simulation management and readiness reporting for hospital systems,
> first responders and government programs.

IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS: this company publishes NO public API. There is no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK, no MCP server, no agent card, no webhook
catalog and no public pricing. Do not attempt to call an endpoint here — the application backend
is private and rejects unauthenticated requests. The only way to engage is the demo request form.
